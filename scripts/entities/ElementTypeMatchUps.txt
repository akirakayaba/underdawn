	These are the elements that players and enemies can have,
	included are there strengths, weaknesses, and status effects they can give
	
	NONE: no strengths, weaknesses, or status effects
		
	FIRE: strong against earth and ice, weak against water, and status effect
			is burning the entity (higher continous damage but lasts less
			compared to poison)
		
	EARTH: strong against lightning, weak against wind and poison, and status
			effect is tremor (delays attack)
		
	WATER: strong against fire, weak against lightning and ice, and status
			effect is drenching the entity (movement is slippery)
		
	LIGHTNING:	strong against water, weak against earth, and status effect is
					paralyzing the entity (cant attack)
		
	WIND: strong against poison and earth, weak against ice, and status effect
			is blowing away the entity (they get knocked back farther)
		
	ICE: strong against wind and water, weak against fire, and status effect is
			freezing the entity (cant move)
		
	POISON: strong against earth, weak against wind and lightning, and status
				effect is poisoning the entity (lower continous damage but 
				lasts longer compared to fire)
